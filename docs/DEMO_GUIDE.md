# Synthetic Demo Guide

All scenarios in this guide are invented for demonstrating the prototype. They are not real customer cases, HDFC Bank rules, regulatory thresholds, or evidence of wrongdoing.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. **Routine salary and household bills** — expected baseline; the phrase engine should not invent a typology.
2. **Marketplace collection with a stated business purpose** — demonstrate why multiple incoming payments need context and a documented business model.
3. **Multiple unrelated senders and rapid onward movement** — discuss potential pass-through and mule indicators, then examine relationships, purpose, source of funds and supporting evidence.
4. **Repeated smaller cash deposits** — discuss possible structuring indicators without assuming a threshold or intent.
5. **Funds routed through several accounts** — reconstruct the flow and understand the purpose of intermediaries.
6. **Possible circular movement** — map whether funds return to the originator or connected party and test legitimate explanations.

## Demo discipline

- Start with the user's description as an unverified scenario, not as established fact.
- Separate observable transaction facts from interpretations.
- Ask what evidence would confirm or weaken each hypothesis.
- Test alternative explanations before reaching a view.
- Never enter names, account numbers, identity data, case IDs or confidential narratives.
- Do not use this prototype to decide whether to file a SAR/STR, restrict an account, or close a case.
